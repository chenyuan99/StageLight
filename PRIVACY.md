# StageLight Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 29, 2026**

StageLight is designed as a private, local-first theatre diary.

## Data collection

StageLight does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It does not include advertising or third-party tracking.

## Data stored on your device

Shows, performances, ratings, notes, venue details, and photos you add are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app's diary, collection, search, filtering, and statistics features.

## Camera and photo library

StageLight requests camera or photo-library access only when you choose to capture or select an image. Selected images are stored with your local diary. You can manage these permissions in iPhone Settings.

## Accounts

StageLight does not require or provide user accounts.

## Children

Because StageLight does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If StageLight's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released.
