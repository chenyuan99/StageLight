# StageLight Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 3, 2026**

StageLight is designed as a private theatre diary that stores your collection
on your device and, when iCloud is available, syncs it through your private
iCloud account.

## Data collection

The developer of StageLight does not collect, sell, or share personal data.
StageLight does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking.

## Data stored on your device

Shows, performances, ratings, notes, venue details, and photos you add are
stored on your device. This information is used only to provide the app's
diary, collection, search, filtering, and statistics features.

## iCloud sync

When you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is available, StageLight uses
Apple's CloudKit service to sync your collection through your private iCloud
account. The developer does not operate a StageLight server and cannot access
your private iCloud data. You can manage iCloud access in Apple device
settings.

## Camera and photo library

StageLight requests camera or photo-library access only when you choose to
capture or select an image. Selected images are stored with your diary and may
sync through your private iCloud account. You can manage these permissions in
iPhone Settings.

Text recognition for imported images is performed on device.

## Apple Maps

The optional theatre search uses Apple Maps. When you use it, search text and
location-related requests are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
StageLight saves only the theatre and city details you choose for your diary.
You can always enter these details manually instead.

## Accounts

StageLight does not require or provide user accounts.

## Children

Because StageLight does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If StageLight's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released.
