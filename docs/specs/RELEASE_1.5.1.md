# StageLight 1.5.1 — Live

The app owner confirmed that 1.5.1 is fully live on the App Store on 2026-10-20.
This is an availability confirmation date, not a claim about the exact release
timestamp. The build number and patch-specific changes are not recorded here
because they have not been verified.

- Platforms: iPhone and iPad.
- Languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Japanese.
- Download or update: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6806575225
- Landing page: https://yuanchen.io/StageLight/

## Current product highlights

- Optional Mini Theatre bunny companion, local prop collection, and stage decoration.
- Share bunny outfit images without nickname or diary details.
- Golden Premiere default app icon; Original, Theatre bunny, and Cream bunny alternatives in Profile.
- Existing diary records and private iCloud settings remain unchanged.
- Mini Theatre progress stays on the device and does not sync through iCloud.
- The Golden Premiere cat shown in the website logo wall is a design concept, not a selectable app icon.
