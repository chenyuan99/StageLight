# StageLight 1.1.0

StageLight now keeps your theatre memories in sync with iCloud and makes theatre entry faster with optional Apple Maps search.

## What’s New

- Sync performances across your Apple devices with iCloud.
- Automatically migrate existing on-device performances without deleting or replacing them.
- See iCloud sync status from the Profile screen.
- Find theatres with Apple Maps when adding or editing a performance.
- Continue entering theatre and city details manually whenever you prefer.

This release also includes reliability improvements and expanded test coverage.
